### Summary
Walter Isaacson's Elon Musk is an intimate, two-year-shadowed biography exploring the complex, driven billionaire behind Tesla, SpaceX, and Twitter. It portrays Musk as a tortured visionary propelled by a harsh South African childhood, an addiction to risk, and a "demon mode" that sparks massive innovation while alienating those close to him.

### What the book is about
- Childhood and Psychology: Examines how severe bullying and a complicated relationship with his father created a relentless "demon mode" and a constant craving for drama.
- Major Companies: Details the inner workings and high-stakes environments of Tesla, SpaceX, and his acquisition of Twitter (now X).
- Leadership Style: Explores his intense management tactics, obsession with engineering first principles, and tendency to push teams to extreme limits.
- The Core Question: Investigates whether the personal flaws and ruthless intensity that drive Musk are the very same traits required to push humanity forward into new technological frontiers

Walter Isaacson charts Elon Musk’s journey from humble beginnings to one of the wealthiest people on the planet – but is Musk a genius or a jerk?
