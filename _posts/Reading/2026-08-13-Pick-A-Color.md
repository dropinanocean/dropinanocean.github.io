### Summary
<em>Pick a color</em>  is a prize-winning 2025 novel by Souvankham Thammavongsa. Set during a single summer day, it follows Ning, a retired boxer and nail salon owner whom privileged customers only know by the generic name "Susan". The book explores class, labor, and the immigrant experience through Ning's sharp inner observations.

### What the book is about
- Identity and Invisibility: Privileged clients see the workers as nameless, interchangeable "Susans," ignoring Ning's rich intellect and past.
- The Power of Labor: The book examines the rhythms of daily work, beauty routines, and the delicate power dynamics between workers and clients.
- A Boxer's Mindset: Ning applies lessons and discipline from her past as a prizefighter to how she observes the world around her.

Set over a single ordinary day at a nail salon, the narrator Ning—a former boxer who goes by "Susan"—reaches a poignant final moment of recognition and intimacy with her rival/frenemy Rachel, finding quiet dignity, acceptance, and self-worth in her life and labor rather than outward dramatic transformation.
