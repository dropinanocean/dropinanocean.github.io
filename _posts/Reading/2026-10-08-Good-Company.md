### Summary
Good Company by Cynthia D'Aprix Sweeney is a character-driven novel exploring twenty-year marriage, hidden secrets, and the complexities of lifelong friendship.

### What the book is about
- The Discovery: Flora Mancini has been happily married to Julian for over two decades, and they have a teenage daughter named Ruby. On the night of Ruby’s high school graduation party, Flora finds an envelope in a garage filing cabinet containing Julian’s wedding ring.
- The Lie: Julian had claimed the ring was lost years earlier while swimming during a specific summer in Manhattan and Los Angeles, when they were struggling to keep Julian's small theater company—Good Company—afloat.
- The Shift to LA: After years of scraping by in New York, the family eventually relocated to Los Angeles, where Julian landed a steady TV role and Flora became a voice-over actor for an animated series.
- The Friendships: The story also revolves around Flora’s best friend, Margot, a successful television star who shares a deep history with Julian and Flora from their early theater days. Margot is also dealing with personal strains, including her husband David suffering a stroke.
